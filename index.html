    Gregorio Luperón International Airport (POP)
    ⚡ Fastest-growing airport in DR

    Puerto Plata is on the move

    After recovering from pandemic-era lows, Gregorio Luperón International Airport posted the highest year-over-year growth of any airport in the Dominican Republic in 2024 — a 20% surge to 880K passengers. With Miami, Toronto, and New York JFK as its top routes, the north coast is drawing significant North American and Canadian traffic.

    Key Insights

    🚀
    Record-breaking 2024
    The DR welcomed 11.2M visitors in 2024 — the highest in its history — with air arrivals up 32% vs pre-pandemic 2019 and cruise traffic up 141%.
    ✈️
    Puerto Plata leading growth
    POP posted the fastest airport growth in the country at +20% YoY, recovering from 387K passengers in 2021 to 880K in 2024 — a 127% climb in just 3 years.
    💰
    $10.9B economic engine
    Tourism represents 8.8% of DR's GDP — the highest tourism-to-GDP ratio in Latin America after El Salvador, surpassing Colombia, Brazil, and Panama in absolute revenue.
    📈
    2025: Another record year
    Jan–Aug 2025 alone saw 8M+ visitors — a 49% surge vs the same period in 2019. Full-year revenue is projected to surpass $11B for the first time.
    🚢
    Cruise tourism exploding
    Cruise arrivals grew 141% compared to 2019 and 18% over 2023. The DR is on track to break the 3M cruise passenger barrier, cementing its position as a Caribbean cruise hub.
    🏨
    Capacity expanding fast
    With 87,322 hotel rooms and 10,060 more in construction across 42 projects, the DR is aggressively building capacity to support the tourism boom.
